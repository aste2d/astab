astab

About

A calm new tab for your open tabs, bookmarks and focused time.

Created and maintained by aste2d ↗ · Version 2.1.2

Features

Open tabs on the left, search and bookmarks on the right. Drag cards directly to reorder them; order is saved automatically. Each tab has a Copy title button. Saved pages, todos/notes and a compact Pomodoro sit along the right edge.

  • Browse bookmark folders in their original Chrome order, use breadcrumbs to return, or search across folders. Bookmarks are never edited.
  • Pomodoro: 25-minute focus and 5/15-minute breaks, with pause, resume and reset. The timer survives page closure and sleep. No system notifications.

Auto close extras is on by default. Each new tab closes all other new-tab pages across all windows, including pinned new-tab pages. Ordinary webpages remain open. You can disable this in settings.

Privacy

Read the full Privacy Policy, including local data use, retention, clipboard actions and Limited Use.

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

astab has no account, analytics, advertising or developer-operated server. It does not send your tabs, bookmark URLs, saved pages, todos or timer activity to aste2d.

Settings, saved items, timer state, and background images are stored locally in Chrome’s extension storage. Bookmarks remain in Chrome’s own bookmark system, including any sync you have configured in Chrome. Removing the extension removes its local settings; it does not delete your Chrome bookmarks.

Network connections

  • Forest wallpapers are on by default. A curated collection of eight Unsplash forest photos rotates daily and supports Next photo. Image requests go to images.unsplash.com. The collection repeats after cycling; this is not a live search or official Unsplash API integration.
  • Optional Daily discovery requests public photo metadata and images from picsum.photos and fastly.picsum.photos. Providers receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address and requested photo identifier. Requests omit credentials and referrers; no tabs, bookmarks or saved items are included.
  • Your latest downloaded photo is cached locally. Offline or failed requests keep the currently displayed background; if no image is available, the original solid theme remains. Your custom background is stored separately. Choose My background / Solid color to disable online wallpaper requests.
  • Website icons use Chrome’s favicon service. Searches go through your configured Chrome search provider. Opening a link connects to its destination normally.

Why these permissions?

  • tabs: read open-page titles and URLs to show groups and perform requested tab actions.
  • bookmarks: display and search your Chrome bookmarks and folders. astab uses read-only bookmark operations, although Chrome labels this permission “read and change.”
  • storage: keep preferences, saved items, backgrounds and timer state on this device.
  • search: use your existing default search engine.
  • favicon: display cached site icons.
  • alarms: finish a running timer even when its page is closed.

astab requests no website host permissions, clipboard-reading permission, browsing-history permission or remote executable code.
